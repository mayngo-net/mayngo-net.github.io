The writing process can be a lot like walking through a forest. You can feel stuck or lost. You might even be hesitant to enter into it.

What is inside the forest? Many things like thoughts, memories, emotions, all the things unsaid that we want to say. Or simply a story we really want to tell.

And sometimes, you just need a guide to get you through it.

We will work together to develop a map of sorts. A map that is unique to you, in your current season of life. It will be flexible – because sometimes you’ll want to stop and play in the forest, sometimes you’ll need a cool drink of water before you can start again. Lives are busy and complex. But with a little support, you CAN get there. I know, because I’ve been through it, many, many times before.

Going into the forest is essentially about connecting with your writer self.  And maybe you are hesitant about going in alone.
